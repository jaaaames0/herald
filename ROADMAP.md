# Herald — Roadmap

**Feature freeze (from 2026-10-17).** The 2026-10 reset rebuilt Herald as a
feed-first digest (see DESIGN.md). Nothing new is built until James has read it
daily for a week and judged it against the "working" criteria in DESIGN.md.

During the week, only fixes: broken collection, failed digests, rendering bugs,
and prompt corrections justified by the eval or James's notes. (Story
continuity was brought forward from the list below on his first note.)

## After the week (only if it's being read)

Candidates, in order, each to be justified by the eval and James's notes:

1. Read the linked article for the top few stories (the "dig in" James wanted),
   within the per-digest budget.
2. Tune selection from what James says he missed or skipped.

Out of scope until X works: multi-source (RSS/news), email/push, feedback UI.

Phases before 2026-10 (3.5–3.8.x, story desk, editions) are superseded; see
git history and CHANGELOG.
